How OPPORA works

From announcement to the right students — automatically.

Admins publish once

Clubs, lecturers and departments submit opportunities through one standard form.

AI extracts the details

Skills, category and experience level are suggested automatically — the admin reviews and confirms.

The engine matches

Eligibility rules first, then a weighted score: skills 50%, interests 25%, academics 15%, experience 10%.

Students get told why

A clear match explanation, skill gaps to grow, and reminders before deadlines.
