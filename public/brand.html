Campus After Dark.

OPPORA feels like launch night on campus: a deep cobalt sky, sodium-amber windows, a coral sunset on the horizon and a few hundred drones drawing shapes overhead. Up close it's warm paper, ink outlines and stickers — handmade, a bit cheeky, never corporate.

Palette

Pulled straight from the APU campus photo at dusk and the crest's red, yellow, blue and green. Coral is the action colour; amber is for highlights; ink draws every outline.

Type

Three voices: a quirky display face for shouting, a friendly sans for reading, and handwriting for the little asides a real designer scribbles in the margin.

Headings
Bricolage Grotesque

Chunky, slightly wonky letterforms with real personality — fun at 80px, still clean at 18px. Its optical sizes keep big headlines tight.

Body
Figtree

Round, open and very readable on phones. Friendly enough to sit next to Bricolage without fighting it.

Accents
Caveat

Handwritten notes, arrows and labels — used sparingly so it feels like a human touch, not a theme.

Logo directions

Three routes. A is used across the prototype; B and C are ready to swap in. None of them copies the APU crest — A only nods to its orbit, so the two sit happily side by side.

A · Orbit O in use

The O of OPPORA is a little planet. The coral core is you; the green comet on the amber orbit is an opportunity heading your way. A playful nod to the orbit around the APU globe, and it animates naturally — the drones draw it in the sky.

B · Spark Pin

A hand-cut map pin with a spark inside: the opportunity lands right where you are. The most "app icon" of the three — bold coral, readable at 16px, great as a favicon and on merch stickers.

C · Constellation O

Students as stars joined into an O; the big amber star is you. Mirrors the drone show and the matching idea (connecting people) — the most poetic option, best on dark backgrounds.

House rules

✓ Do

  • Ink outlines + hard offset shadows (stickers, not glows)
  • Tilt things a degree or two — nothing perfectly square
  • Halftone dots instead of gradients on colour blocks
  • Bouncy hover, satisfying press-down on click
  • One handwritten note per screen, max

✕ Don't

  • Purple-to-blue gradients or soft glassy cards
  • Plain centred boxes on a white page
  • Animations that block typing or clicking
  • Motion for people who asked for reduced motion
  • Copying the APU crest — keep it as a partner mark
